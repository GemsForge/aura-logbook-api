The changes

1

Moon leads step 1. A 70–76px disc with the phase as the title. The sign card shrinks to a title, the reading and a small glyph.

2

Every step ends with "Next". Moving forward no longer depends on the rail, and "Skip" keeps every step optional.

3

Today is marked and the strip is explained. It opens on "Today", with a fade and an "Earlier days" cue, plus a one-line dot legend.

4

Desktop sky summary. The right rail keeps the selected day's moon in view across steps, as the original web wireframe planned.

5

One check-in. Mood buttons end step 3, and the duplicate "Reflect with a mood check-in" fold-out is removed from this page.

✓

Already shipped locally: the extra titles are hidden, the strip is shorter and opens on today, and the horoscope card is smaller.
